# v1.11 — 2026-08-11: Server-side trailing stops + liquidity floor

**Status: NOT yet validated. Requires a clean paper week before funding.**

## The finding that drove this release

**The v1.8/v1.9 trailing stop NEVER fired in live trading.** Zero TRAIL lines
in any log Aug 3–6, despite CWVX peaking +3.88% and INTC +3.19% on Aug 4 —
both far past the arming threshold. Root cause: `_check_trailing` ratcheted
via `broker.move_stop()`, whose `replace_order_by_id` calls failed on every
attempt and were swallowed by `except: pass` — no log line, no retry alarm.

Consequences, scored against VALIDATION-GO-NOGO.md:

- **Hard fail #2/#7:** CWVX (Aug 4) peaked +3.88% and still exited at the
  full initial stop (−1.88%). The exact failure mode the trail was added to
  kill, criterion #7's one-strike NO-GO.
- **Hard fail #1/#3:** AHCO carried overnight Aug 4→5 and lost −5.39%
  (~3.6× intended risk).
- Verdict for the Aug 3–7 week: **NO-GO** (also: no log Fri Aug 7 — bot not
  started, so the sample was short anyway).

This also reframes the Aug-10 hotfix: Aug 4–6 wasn't primarily "stop too
tight" — it was winners unprotected by a broken trail. With a working trail,
CWVX (+3.88% peak) locks ~+2.5% instead of −1.88%; SNAP/INSM (~+1.5% peaks)
exit ~flat instead of full stops.

## Changes

1. **Server-side trailing stops** (`runner._check_trailing`,
   `broker.activate_trailing_stop`): once a position's peak is far enough
   past breakeven that a `trail_pct` trail locks ≥ breakeven, the bot cancels
   the bracket legs and submits a native Alpaca **trailing-stop order**
   (`trail_percent: 1.25`). The high-water mark is then tracked server-side
   tick by tick — no 30s poll lag, no replace-order ratchet. One handoff per
   position, logged as `TRAIL ACTIVATED`; fills detected and logged as
   `TRAIL EXIT` (bookkeeping classifies these as `trail`). If the handoff
   fails, a plain stop is restored at the original level and the failure is
   logged loudly; retried next loop.
2. **No more silent order-management failures:** `move_stop` now logs every
   listing/replace failure (this silence is what hid the broken trail for
   four trading days).
3. **Liquidity floor** (`screener.min_prev_day_volume: 2,000,000` shares):
   candidates whose previous day traded fewer shares are skipped and logged
   (`ILLIQUID skipped ...`). AHCO's overnight disaster and CWVX's
   3-attempt/partial-fill entry were both thin-name problems.

Tests: trailing-stop unit test rewritten for the handoff semantics; 26/26
pass.

## RESOLVED same day: stop_pct reverted 2.5 → 1.5

Ethan ran the deciding sweep (Jun 8 – Aug 8, 230 sim trades, no-shorts,
bias 0.5, trail 1.25, arm breakeven, tp off) — monotonic against the
hotfix's wider stop:

| stop | net P&L | exp/trade | max DD | win rate |
|-----:|--------:|----------:|-------:|---------:|
| 1.5  | +268.33 |     +1.17 |    222 |    52.2% |
| 2.0  | +150.80 |     +0.66 |    250 |    55.7% |
| 2.5  |  +32.27 |     +0.14 |    334 |    57.8% |
| 3.0  |  −64.96 |     −0.28 |    382 |    59.6% |

2.5 gave up ~88% of the edge and took 50% more drawdown. Wider stops buy
win rate by turning small losses into big ones — the wrong trade once the
trail protects winners. `stop_pct: 1.5` restored in config.yaml. Note the
tighter stop feeds sizing (risk 1%/stop% → notional; notional cap binds ⇒
effective risk ≈ 0.5% of equity/trade).
