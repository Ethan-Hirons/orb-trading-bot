# E3 ANSWERED — there is no paper→live fill penalty

Data: `state/trade_history.csv` (paper, 150 trades) and
`live/state/trade_history.csv` (live, 33 trades), both synced from the VPS
2026-10-22 via the new `sync-both.ps1`. Live had never been synced since
2026-10-12; this is the first side-by-side look.

Nothing deployed. No config, `state/` or `orb_bot/` change.

---

## 1. E3 — the result

`EXPERIMENTS.md` recorded the prior as: *"every baseline number is a paper
number, and Alpaca's paper engine fills optimistically, so the honest prior is
that live underperforms paper on identical signals before any edge question."*

**That prior is wrong.** Measured on 31 matched pairs (same date, same symbol,
both books, WFF excluded and treated separately below):

| | mean | median |
|---|---|---|
| entry price diff (live − paper) | +0.0134% | −0.0086% |
| P&L% delta (live − paper) | **−0.0038 pp** | +0.0200 pp |

stdev of the delta 0.231 pp, so SE = 0.042 pp and the 95% interval is roughly
**[−0.086, +0.079] pp per trade**. Any systematic live penalty is smaller than
0.09 pp per trade — against a 1.5% stop, that is noise.

**Why this is a strong result and the selection findings were not:** this is a
*paired* design. Same signal, same symbol, same minute, two accounts. It is not
100 trades cut into buckets of 15, so there is no subset to overfit and no two
trades to drop.

**The one real divergence is WFF 2026-10-12**, the +21% mover: live exited
3.98% below paper (+21.7% vs +26.7%). Fills agree on ordinary trades and come
apart on violent ones. Expect paper to overstate the tail, not the body.

**Consequence:** the 150-trade paper book is usable evidence about live
behaviour. That was not previously established.

## 3. Live is not outperforming — it is undercapitalised

Live position sizes are a median **0.641×** paper's (equity 1511.07 vs
2090.84). It therefore runs out of buying power and takes fewer positions.

- Paper took **17** trades live did not, totalling **−49.21**
- Live took **1** paper did not

So live "avoided" −49.21 of losses. That is a capital constraint, not
selection. The same constraint cost live AXTX (+24.36) and CRWV (+33.75) on
10-20 — the two best trades of that day. n=17 with two trades deciding the
sign: a subset finding, and the meta-lesson applies. **Do not read live's
headline expectancy as evidence the live instance picks better.**

## 5. What this changes

1. **Close E3.** Answered, in the direction opposite to the prior. Paper fills
   are a fair proxy for live fills on ordinary trades.
2. **Do not treat "paper went positive" as a result.** Ex-top-2 it did not.
3. **E8 is still unbuilt** — no `rejected_breakouts` code in the repo. 170
   rejections across 64 paper sessions (52.3% of all breakouts) have been
   logged as events and discarded as outcomes. The symbol, side and timestamp
   are recoverable from the logs; only the subsequent price action is missing,
   and that is retrievable from Alpaca historical bars.
