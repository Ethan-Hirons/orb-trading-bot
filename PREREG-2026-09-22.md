## 2. Where the losses come from

Clean regime only: long-only, 1.5% stop, native trail live (08-13 → 09-22).

| exit | n | mean R | net $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| stop | 17 | −1.06 | **−185.64** |
| trail | 17 | +0.84 | +141.62 |
| eod-flatten | 8 | +0.23 | +19.50 |
| **total** | 42 | −0.05 | −24.52 |

Stops are the entire loss. You cannot remove them — a breakout system that
never gets stopped isn't using a stop. The only levers are **how often** a
trade gets stopped and **how much** each stop costs, and those trade off
against each other.

## 3. What the stopped trades have in common (exploratory)

**This section is data-mined.** 13 splits × 2 outcomes = 26 tests on 42
trades; nothing survives a Bonferroni correction (best p = 0.128). Per
`sweep-results-log` Run 6: "selecting the best-performing ~40% of trades by
any criterion correlated with outcome will look clean." Treat everything
below as hypothesis, not finding.

| bucket (clean regime) | n | stop rate | mean R | net $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stock, news-confirmed bias | 12 | 25% | +0.52 | +58.05 |
| stock, no directional news (`any`) | 8 | 50% | −0.33 | −26.65 |
| leveraged ETP, no directional news | 12 | 58% | −0.35 | −41.97 |
| crypto ETF, all | 9 | 33% | −0.17 | −14.98 |

News-confirmed vs `any`, all instruments: **+0.41R vs −0.30R**, stop rate
27% vs 48%. Bootstrap 95% CI on the R gap: +0.05 to +1.38. 64% of clean-regime
trades were `any`.

Older eras point the same way once 08-03 (the broken-trail artifact) is
excluded: 3%-stop era +0.10 vs −0.03, 1.5% pre-trail era −0.66 vs −1.36. Those
eras ran different configs, so that is weak corroboration, not replication.

Two mechanisms that predate this analysis, which is why these two are worth a
test at all:

- **Leveraged ETPs are news-blind by construction.** `news.py` queries
  `NewsRequest(symbols=symbol)` — the ETP's own ticker. Articles are written
  about SNDK, not SNXX. 26 of 34 leveraged-ETP trades in the whole trial had
  zero articles. SNXX has been traded 8 times and has never had one.
- **A fixed 1.5% stop sits inside the opening range.** Median opening range is
  4.0% for stocks and 5.0% for leveraged ETPs. Stopped trades had an OR 3.05x
  the stop width; trades that weren't stopped, 2.10x. On a 2x ETP a 1.5% stop
  is ~0.75% on the underlying. (`backtest.py` already flagged this, v1.12.)

Power: to detect a 27% vs 48% stop rate at 80% power needs **~82 trades per
group**. At the live rate that is months, not a week. The live forward test
below is a sanity check; the sim is the test.

## 4. Pre-registered hypotheses

### H1 — Require a news-confirmed direction

**Claim:** skipping targets whose bias is `any` (no directional news, which
includes nearly every leveraged ETP) raises expectancy per trade.

**Sim test:** ONE run, config exactly as live, A = as-is, B = A but targets
with bias `any` are not traded. Window **2025-07-01 → 2025-12-31** — no ORB
sweep has used it (ORB sweeps used 2026-01-02 → 06-05 and 2026-06-08 →
08-08; Track B's 2024–26 window was QQQ-only). Needs a `--require-news` flag
in `sweep.py` (sim code, not bot code).

**Pass = all four:**
1. B expectancy/trade > A expectancy/trade
2. B expectancy > 0 with t ≥ 2.0
3. B stop-out rate < A stop-out rate
4. B max drawdown ≤ A max drawdown

Fail any → H1 rejected. No second window, no threshold tweak, no re-cut.

### H2 — Stop at the opening-range low (classic ORB), same dollar risk

**Claim:** placing the stop at the OR low (the bot's existing, never-tested
`exits.mode: range`) instead of a flat 1.5% cuts stop-outs by more than it
raises the cost of each one.

**Critical detail:** today the $700 notional cap binds, so a stop really
risks ~$10.50 (≈0.5% of equity), not the configured 1%. A wider stop with
`risk_per_trade_pct: 1.0` would silently **double** the dollar loss per stop.
So B must run with `risk_per_trade_pct: 0.5`, and `take_profit_r` raised to
an unreachable value (e.g. 100) so the trail — not a 1R target — stays the
upside exit, matching live.

**Sim test:** same window and the same four criteria, A = live, B = range
mode as above. Needs a `--exit-mode range` flag in `sweep.py`.

### Rules for both

- Run H1 and H2 **once each**, separately. If both pass, run B1+B2 combined
  once on the same window as a third, confirmatory check — do not stack them
  on the strength of the separate results (that is exactly Run 5).
- Sim fidelity caveat stands: the sim overstated live trade count 3.3x and
  P&L by ~+96 over Jun–Aug. A pass means "worth a paper trial," not "edge."
- **Live forward check** from 2026-09-23: `python loss_anatomy.py --since
  2026-09-23` weekly. No decision from it until each bucket has 30+ trades.
- Nothing here ships to the live account without a clean paper week on the
  changed config first (`VALIDATION-GO-NOGO.md` still binds).

## 5. Risk items for real money (not strategy — do before funding)

- **Stop-limit has no fill guarantee.** `stop_limit: true`, offset 0.5%. If
  price gaps through the limit the order sits unfilled and the position is
  unprotected. AHCO 08-05 realized −5.39% on a −1.5% stop (−3.6R), muddied by
  that day's ops bug but the order-type risk is real. Options: stop-market, or
  a watchdog that market-closes if a stop triggered and the position is still
  open after N seconds. Config change → after Friday.
- **Auth failures crash-loop instead of alerting.** 11 crashes on 401s today.
  On a live day that is a missed session, or positions managed only by their
  broker-side brackets.
- **`start.sh` on the VPS is patched in place.** `vps-setup.sh` sed-rewrites
  `python3` to the venv interpreter, so the server's `start.sh` always shows
  as locally modified. Any `git stash` without a `pop`, or a `git checkout --
  deploy/start.sh`, silently reverts it to system `python3` (no alpaca-py) and
  the next 09:25 start fails. Fix after Friday: have `start.sh` use
  `.venv/bin/python` when it exists and drop the sed. Until then, don't commit
  the server's version to the repo, and always `stash pop` after a pull.
- **`trade_features.csv` header is stale** (17 fields, rows since 08-13 have
  18). `report.py` should rewrite the header when fields change. Bot code →
  after Friday. `loss_anatomy.py` works around it.
