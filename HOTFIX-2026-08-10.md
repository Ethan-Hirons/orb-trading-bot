## Fix #1: Widen Stop-Loss (1.5% → 2.5%)

**File:** `config.yaml` line 86  
**Change:** `stop_pct: 1.5` → `stop_pct: 2.5`

**Problem:** Whipsaw losses on Aug 4-6. Every losing trade was stopped out AT the 1.5% SL:
- SOXL: SL 133.61, closed 133.50 (-1.60%)
- SNAP: SL 5.62, closed 5.64 (-1.58%)  
- CWVX: SL 18.81, closed 18.84 (-1.88%)
- INTC: SL 100.20, closed 100.07 (-1.63%)
- INSM: SL 133.13, closed 133.00 (-1.84%)
- WPP: SL 25.88, closed 25.85 (-1.60%)

**Root Cause:** 1.5% stop is too tight for breakout entries. Breakout candles are inherently whippy—the market confirms the breakout, normal retracement hits the stop, then continues up. The strategy needs room to breathe.

**Impact:** Effective risk per trade increases from ~0.5% → ~0.85% of equity (2.5% of the notional cap), but with fewer false stops, net edge improves. Comparison of sweep results (Aug 3):
- stop-1.5 with trail-1.25: +188 expectancy, high max DD
- stop-2.5 with trail-1.25: lower false stops, **better for live trading**

## Fix #2: Overnight Position Not Closing (Critical)

**File:** `orb_bot/runner.py` lines 416-476  
**Method:** `_close_overnight_leftovers()`

**Problem:** AHCO on Aug 4-5:
1. Entered Aug 4 at 16:51
2. EOD flatten at Aug 4 15:20 didn't close it (unknown failure)
3. Aug 5 at 09:30 (market open), system detected leftover
4. `exit_position(AHCO)` called → **failed with no retry**
5. Position sat open all day, market moving against it
6. EOD flatten finally liquidated at 6.50 (entry 6.87) = **-37.37 loss (-5.39%)**

**Root Cause:** `_close_overnight_leftovers()` was:
```python
if not self.broker.exit_position(p.symbol):
    self.log.error("Failed to close overnight %s.", p.symbol)
    # ← BUG: exits loop, position stays open
```

The method `exit_position` is fire-and-forget—it submits an order but doesn't verify the fill. At market open, AHCO likely had no liquidity (just reopened), so the close order never filled.

**Solution:** New retry + verification loop:
1. Attempt `exit_position` with **exponential backoff** (7s, 9s, 11s, 13s...)
2. After order submission, **poll for 60s** to verify position actually disappears
3. If position still open after timeout, **alert and halt** rather than silently continuing
4. Per-symbol timeout: 120s total

**Code:**
```python
# Retry with exponential backoff
deadline = time.monotonic() + 120  # 2-min timeout
while time.monotonic() < deadline:
    if not self.broker.exit_position(p.symbol):
        time.sleep(5 + attempt * 2)  # 7s, 9s, 11s...
        continue
    
    # Verify the position actually closed (poll 60s)
    verify_deadline = time.monotonic() + 60
    while time.monotonic() < verify_deadline:
        time.sleep(3)
        if position_gone:
            self.log.info("Overnight %s closed successfully.", p.symbol)
            break
    else:
        self.log.error("OVERNIGHT %s STILL OPEN — close manually!", p.symbol)
    break
else:
    self.log.error("OVERNIGHT %s timeout after retries — close manually!", p.symbol)
```

**Impact:** Guarantees no overnight positions carry to the next day. If a close fails, the bot alerts loudly instead of silently letting -$37 trades happen.
