fill, not the trail working as designed. The same mechanism pointed the other
way would have produced a loss far beyond the 1.5% stop.

**The spread gate did work as written**: the 10:00:44 check saw 11.61% and

## 2. The finding that matters most: the trial IS its outliers

| | n | net | ex-top2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| paper, all trades | 124 | **+38.10** | **−229.68** (−1.88/trade) |
| live, all trades | 13 | +67.37 | ex-WFF −25.84 |

Paper's two best trades (WFF 10-12 +183.42, SNXX 08-03 +84.36) are worth
+267.78. Everything else is −229.68. Every feature present on WFF was checked
for "more days like this":

| bucket | n | net | without WFF |
|---|---|---|---|
| gap ≥ 50% | 12 | +152.25 | −31.17, 3 winners in 11 |
| opening rel-vol ≥ 5x | 11 | +166.89 | −16.53 |
| positive news sentiment | 56 | +162.15 | −21.27 |

**Nothing selects for WFF without WFF.** The data cannot predict which day
produces the outlier. What it supports is (a) staying exposed to outliers —
don't filter out the extreme names — and (b) losing less on everything else.
That is exactly what E12 tests.

**Not recommended, written down so it is not re-proposed after the next big
day:** larger size on extreme gappers, loosening `max_spread_pct`, raising
`max_abs_gap_pct` (75). Each raises outlier exposure *and* gap-through-stop
exposure by the same mechanism, and n=1.

## 4. Data-hygiene issue found today

`live/state/trade_features.csv` has **two rows for WFF 10-12** — the
rolled-back entry (10:01:52, never filled) wrote a feature row as well as the
real one (10:06:05). Feature rows appear to be written at ENTER, before fill
confirmation. Any E2 / relvol analysis read from `trade_features.csv` should
join on `trade_history.csv` or drop rows whose entry was rolled back
(`ENTRY ROLLED BACK` in the log). Paper has no such rows yet (its rollbacks
were 07-28 / 07-29 / 08-04, before the file existed).

## 6. Proposed new item — E13: know when the bot is blind (measurement only)

**Observation.** The bot trades on the IEX feed (`data_feed: iex`, free
plan). IEX carries a few percent of volume; on a name trading ~300 shares a
day it can go minutes without a print. Today that put the bot's equity,
MFE and trail-arming peak minutes and ~27 percentage points behind the tape.
The fills themselves happen on the consolidated tape, so the bot is managing a
position it cannot see.

**Change (logging only, no decision logic):** for each trade, record the
number of IEX trades during the hold and flag any `Halted` headline for the
symbol that session; at EOD, compare logged MFE against SIP MFE from
`replay.py`'s cache. **Question, answered at n ≥ 50:** how many trades are
"blind" (few IEX prints during the hold), and do their outcomes differ from
the rest? No threshold proposed. If blind trades turn out to be most of the
outliers *and* most of the gap-through losses, that is the evidence for a
risk rule; today it is one trade.

Needs a change to `orb_bot/runner.py` and a VPS deploy → Ethan's call.
E8 (rejected-breakout logging) is still unimplemented and in the same
position.
