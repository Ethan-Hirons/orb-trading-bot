# PRE-REGISTRATION — stop 1.0 + trail, the holdout it has been waiting for

Written 2026-10-22, **before the holdout was run**. Criteria are not edited
afterwards; a RESULT section is appended.

## Background

On 2026-10-01, five exit candidates were tested on the 38-40 current-regime
trades. Four died. One survived every test:

> **stop 1.0 + trail** — full / ex-top2 / ex-top3 = +17.80 / +17.81 / +17.80
> (touch) and +20.63 / +20.64 / +20.63 (close), both halves same sign in both
> fill modes. Its gain does **not** come from the winners: dropping the top 3
> moves the delta by 0.01. It comes from losing less on the trades that fail.

That note ended: *"in-sample with NO holdout left ... The only remaining test
is forward paper data with pre-registered criteria — there is nothing left to
backtest it on."*

Three weeks of that data now exist.

| set | window | trades |
|---|---|---|
| formed the hypothesis | 2026-08-19 .. 2026-10-01 | 40 |
| **holdout, never touched** | **2026-10-02 .. 2026-10-22** | **48** |

## The test

Replay each of the 48 holdout trades twice through the validated harness:

- **live rule**: `stop_pct 1.5, trail_pct 1.25, trail_arm 1.25`
- **candidate**: `stop_pct 1.0, trail_pct 1.25, trail_arm 1.25`

Delta = candidate − live. Primary unit is **% per trade**, not dollars,
because sizing changed across the window.

**Validation gate first**, as in E8: the harness must reproduce the 48
holdout trades under the live rule at median |error| < 0.25 pp and mean
signed error within ±0.10 pp. **Gate fails → no result is read.**

## PASS requires ALL FIVE

1. delta **positive** on the holdout
2. sign survives **drop-top-2 and drop-top-3**
3. sign holds in **both halves** of the holdout by date
4. sign agrees in **both fill modes** (touch and close)
5. sign agrees on **both feeds** (IEX and SIP)

These mirror the tests it passed in-sample. Anything less is not the same bar.

## What each outcome authorises

| outcome | authorises |
|---|---|
| passes all five | the candidate moves from *untested* to *supported by one out-of-sample holdout* — further than anything else in this project has got. It authorises **proposing** the change to Ethan with a pre-committed kill criterion. It does **not** auto-deploy anything. |
| fails any | **closed.** The 10-01 result was in-sample luck, one of five candidates. No re-running with a different stop value, window or fill mode. |

## Limitations, stated in advance

- **A tighter stop changes position size.** `risk 1%/stop% = notional`, so a
  1.0% stop sizes ~1.5x larger for the same 1% risk. The % per-trade metric
  normalises this; **in dollars the real bot would amplify both directions.**
  Any deployment decision must treat this as a sizing change, not just a stop
  change.
- **Per-trade, not portfolio.** An earlier stop-out frees buying power and
  could enable a later trade the same day. The harness cannot model that.
- n=48, and the in-sample t was ≈0.27-0.67 — never close to significant. A
  pass here is one clean holdout, not proof.
- It was 1 of 5 candidates tried, so some of its in-sample survival is
  multiple comparisons.
- A 1.0% stop means **more** stop-outs, the opposite direction from the
  volatility-normalisation idea.
- The honest prior is still failure: Run 1, Run 5 and Track B all reversed
  sign out-of-sample. 0 for 3.

# RESULT — 2026-10-22

Criteria above untouched.

## VERDICT: DOES NOT PASS as pre-registered. Not deployed, not proposed.

Criteria 4 and 5 required sign agreement across both fill modes and both
feeds. **Three of the four cells failed the validation gate, so they produced
no readable sign.** The bar was not met. This is not a pass.

| cell | median abs err | mean signed err | gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| IEX / touch | 0.047 | +0.098 | **PASS** |
| IEX / close | 0.048 | +0.193 | FAIL |
| SIP / touch | 0.039 | **+0.102** | FAIL (by 0.002) |
| SIP / close | 0.044 | +0.159 | FAIL |

## The one readable cell

IEX/touch — the feed the bot actually sees (`data_feed: iex`) and the
validated decision mode:

| | delta (candidate − live) |
|---|---|
| full, n=44 | **+0.2482 pp/trade** |
| ex-top-2 | +0.2362 |
| ex-top-3 | +0.2297 |
| first half (n=24) | +0.2258 |
| second half (n=20, split 10-14) | +0.2750 |

live mean −0.294% → candidate mean −0.046%. The stop changed the outcome on
28 of 44 trades.

**The robustness signature is the same as in-sample**: dropping the top
three moves the delta by 0.018 pp. The gain is not in the tail. Both halves
positive. Had the other three cells been readable, this would have passed.

**It still does not pass**, because the pre-registered bar was five
conditions and two of them could not be evaluated. Recording it as a pass
would be choosing the criteria after seeing the answer.

## Why the gate failed — one trade

SIP/touch, the near-miss cell: mean error +0.1023 against a ±0.10 gate.

| err | date | symbol | sim exit | booked | sim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **+3.431** | 2026-10-20 | AXTX | trail | +3.513% | +6.944% |
| −0.356 | 2026-10-02 | SDEV | stop | −1.144% | −1.500% |
| +0.317 | 2026-10-16 | FMC | trail | +2.518% | +2.835% |

Excluding the three largest, mean error is **+0.0322 pp** — comfortably
inside. Median |error| is 0.039-0.048 across every cell. **The harness is not
broadly broken; one trail exit on one fast mover is off by 3.4 pp.**

**AXTX is not excluded and the gate is not relaxed.** Both moves would be
choosing the threshold after seeing the result, which is the failure mode
this document exists to prevent.

## What this does and does not authorise

**Does not authorise:** any config change, on paper or live. The candidate
remains untested out-of-sample.

**Does authorise:** fixing the instrument. The AXTX trail divergence is a
specific, diagnosable modelling defect, and it is worth fixing whether or not
this hypothesis is ever revisited. Repairing a measuring device is not
"re-running with different definitions."

**Disclosure for whoever reads this later:** the decision to fix the harness
and re-run was taken *after* seeing a favourable partial result. That is a
real bias risk. Any re-run must be pre-registered afresh with **the gate
unchanged at median < 0.25 / mean within ±0.10**, and must report this
disclosure alongside it.
