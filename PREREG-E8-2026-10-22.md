**Written 2026-10-22, BEFORE any outcome data was fetched or any result
computed.** Nothing below may be edited once `e8_backfill.py` has been run.
A RESULT section gets appended; the criteria are not touched.

Same discipline as `TRACK-B-GONOGO.md`.

## The question

A breakout is taken only if a completed 1-min bar CLOSES beyond the opening
range within the last 3 bars, on volume >= 0.5x the average OR bar
(`confirm_bar_close: true`, `confirm_lookback_bars: 3`,
`volume_filter_ratio: 0.5`).

Across 52 paper sessions the rule **rejects 176 breakouts and admits 155** —
by volume the largest gate in the system, set reactively in v1.5 after one bad
day (2026-07-17) and never swept in Runs 1-6.

**Does it filter out losers, or does it filter out winners?**

## The sample

`state/rejected_breakouts_backfill.csv`, built by `e8_extract.py`.

| | events | sessions |
|---|---|---|
| look window 2026-07-16 .. 2026-09-30 | 124 | 37 |
| holdout 2026-10-01 .. 2026-10-22 | 53 | 15 |
| **total** | **177** | **52** |

164 long / 13 short. Live contributes 1 unique event; the other 40 live
rejections duplicate paper, because live and paper run identical strategy
parameters by design (`config-live.yaml` overrides size and safety only).

## The simulation

For each rejected breakout, simulate taking it with the bot's **current** exit
stack, imposed uniformly on all 177 events:

- opening range = 09:30-09:45 (`opening_range_minutes: 15`), computed from the
  bars, not from the log
- entry at the breakout level: OR high (long) / OR low (short)
- stop 1.5% (`stop_pct`)
- trail 1.25% (`trail_pct`), armed only when trailing from the current price
  still locks >= breakeven — the dead band is reproduced, not idealised
- take-profit 25% — unreachable by design, modelled anyway
- no time stop (`max_hold_minutes: 0`)
- EOD flatten at 15:50 (`flatten_before_close_minutes: 10`)
- entry slippage of +0.046% applied **to both arms** (E1's measured mean)

## VALIDATION GATE — passes before any result is read

The simulator is run over the **155 actually-taken breakouts** and compared to
their booked `pnl_pct`.

**Pass:** median absolute error < 0.25 pp AND mean signed error within
+/-0.10 pp.

**Fail: E8 reports nothing.** The rejected-breakout numbers are not read, not
quoted, and not recorded. `replay.py` was wrong twice behind 22 passing unit
tests; the only thing that ever caught it was validating against reality.

## Primary metric

Mean % return per event, simulated, **rejected vs taken**, over the same
sessions and the same exit stack.

Secondary, reported but not decisive: win rate, payoff ratio, MFE/MAE from the
breakout level, and the share of rejections that never traded through the
level again.

## Robustness bar — all three required

1. **drop-top-2** — sign of the rejected-minus-taken difference holds after
   removing the two best rejected events
2. **split-half by date** — sign holds in both the look window and the holdout
3. **reported in % per event**, never dollars, since sizing differs across the
   window

## What each outcome authorises

| outcome | what it means | what it authorises |
|---|---|---|
| rejected clearly WORSE than taken, robust | the rule earns its place | **nothing changes.** Close E8. |
| rejected clearly BETTER than taken, robust | the rule may be costing money | open **E13**, a proper confirmation-parameter study in the sim with its own holdout. **Does NOT authorise touching `confirm_bar_close` in any config.** |
| fails any robustness check | inconclusive | close E8 inconclusive. **Do not re-run with different definitions, windows or exit assumptions.** |

No result from E8 changes `config.yaml` or `config-live.yaml`. No paper
promotion, no live promotion.

## Known limitations, stated in advance

- **Per-event and unconstrained.** Buying power, `max_trades_per_day` and
  `max_per_underlying` mean the bot could not have taken every rejected
  breakout. The primary metric therefore **overstates** what was actually
  forgone.
- Entry at the level assumes a resting stop order. Real fills slip; the E1
  correction is applied to both arms, which is a partial fix, not a complete
  one.
- 13 shorts is not a sample. The short arm is descriptive only.
- Rejections exist only for symbols that reached the screen that day.
- The 177 events span several config versions. Current config is imposed on
  all of them, and on the comparator too, so the two arms are consistent with
  each other but not with the historical book.

# RESULT — 2026-10-22

Criteria above untouched. Run: `e8_backfill.py`, both feeds, engine reused
from `replay.py`, bars cached in `.replay_cache/`.

## VERDICT: INCONCLUSIVE. E8 closes. Nothing is authorised.

## Validation gate: PASS (both feeds)

| feed | scored | median abs err | mean signed err | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IEX | 141 | 0.098 pp | +0.039 pp | PASS |
| SIP | 148 | 0.093 pp | +0.008 pp | PASS |

The exit engine reproduces trades whose outcome is known. That part is sound
and is reusable.

## Primary metric

| feed | rejected (sim) | taken (sim) | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| IEX | +0.639% (n=75) | +0.801% (n=65) | **−0.162 pp** |
| SIP | +0.465% (n=82) | +0.654% (n=68) | **−0.188 pp** |

## Why it is inconclusive — four independent reasons

**1. The pre-registered sample never existed.** 95 of the 177 events were
rejected AFTER 10:30. `entry_window_minutes` went 180 → 60 on 2026-09-03
(e796004), so those breakouts sit in a window the strategy no longer has.
Jul/Aug: 0 rejections before 10:30. Sep/Oct: 0 after. The look arm is n=29,
not the pre-registered 124.

**2. IEX fails split-half — the sign flips.**

| feed | look 09-08..09-30 | holdout 10-01.. |
|---|---|---|
| IEX | **+0.060 pp** | −0.313 pp |
| SIP | −0.153 pp | −0.221 pp |

IEX is the feed the bot actually sees (`data_feed: iex`). It fails the bar.

**3. The two feeds disagree on the decisive check.** SIP holds sign, IEX does
not. Pre-committed: that disagreement is itself the finding.

**4. The entry assumption is broken.** Entering at the OR level is not
obtainable on fast movers. Actual entry vs OR level on 69 taken trades:
mean **+1.314%**, median +0.332%, stdev 4.72, worst **+38.72%** (WFF
2026-10-12, entered 21.46 against a 15.47 level). Both arms inherit that
fantasy fill, and the rejected arm inherits it on exactly the explosive names
that decide the mean. The +0.046% E1 correction was measured on
confirmation-delayed entries and does not describe a resting stop order.

Per the criteria: **do not re-run with different definitions, windows or exit
assumptions.** E8 is closed.

## What survives

**a. Nothing suggests the confirmation rule costs money.** Every cut is
negative: −0.162 / −0.188, and −0.109 / −0.134 ex-top-2. Weak, but the useful
direction — no action indicated.

**b. The reject rate is stable across the regime change**, so the 53%
headline stands:

| regime | breakouts | taken | rejected | rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 180-min window (..09-03) | 185 | 90 | 95 | 51.4% |
| 60-min window (09-08..) | 150 | 69 | 81 | 54.0% |

**c. The confirmation delay is correlated with the move being real.** Trades
where the bot paid >1% above the breakout level booked **+1.645%** (n=21);
the rest booked **−0.443%** (n=48). Difference +2.088 pp, and **ex-top-2
+0.764 pp — it survives**, which almost nothing in this project does.

Reading: confirmation is behaving as a momentum filter. The names that ran
away from the level before confirming were the names actually moving. This
also explains why (a) comes out the way it does.

**Not a lever.** The gap is only knowable after the move, so it cannot be
traded on. Descriptive only, and it is still a subset finding — the
meta-lesson in [[exit-structure]] applies.

The forward logging change at `runner.py:1094` was **not built**. The question
