## v1.15 (uncommitted → committed today) — two changes

### 1. Buying-power pre-check — unambiguously good

`broker.get_buying_power()` + a check in `_scan_for_entries` before submitting.
Fixes 2026-09-09 SNXX: asked $685.99 against $669.78, failed 3×, got
`BLOCKED SNXX for the day`, and logged an ERROR that read like a bug rather
than a balance limit. With ~$1,993 equity and a ~$700 position cap, only two
concurrent positions ever fit, so the third was **guaranteed** to fail.

Returns `None` on telemetry failure and the caller falls through to let the
broker decide — the v1.11.1 lesson (never block on a failed measurement)
correctly applied. No trade-selection behaviour changes. **Endorsed.**

### 2. Trail anchoring: peak → current price — CORRECT, BUT IT IS A STRATEGY CHANGE

`_check_trailing` previously computed the arming level from `mfe_pct` (the
recorded peak). Alpaca seeds the native trailing stop's high-water mark **from
the price at the moment the order is accepted**, so a peak reconstructed from a
bar high the 30s poll missed has nothing to do with the level actually
protected.

2026-09-09 IRD is the proof: excursion jumped +0.37% → +3.22% off a bar high,
the trail armed, the log claimed "would lock ~+1.93%", and the position
realized **−0.73%** — the handoff replaced a live −1.5% bracket stop with a
trail anchored below breakeven. v1.15 anchors on current price and adds a
`TRAIL DEFERRED` line (once per symbol) for the qualify-but-retraced state.

Tests: `test_trailing_stop` extended with the IRD replay, the price-holds-up
counter-case, and the short mirror. **All 31 test_logic checks pass, and all
seven other test files (19+6+14+10+5+24+sim_exits) pass.**

**Why this still needs a decision.** Arming semantics are exactly what the
sweeps validated (`arm=breakeven`), and `backtest.simulate_symbol_day` line
~236 still trails off `peak`. **Sim and live have diverged again** — the same
class of mismatch `TUNING-ANALYSIS-2026-07-29` flagged as CRITICAL ("the sweep
endorsed a rule v1.8 doesn't implement"). Every sweep verdict behind the live
`stop 1.5 / trail 1.25` config assumed peak-anchored arming.

**What bounds the risk:** since current ≤ peak by construction, v1.15 arms a
**strict subset** of what v1.14 armed. Verified across cases: it never arms
where v1.14 would not; it only declines in the states that were locking losses.
So it cannot introduce new behaviour, only withhold arming. Expect **fewer
trail exits and more full-stop / EOD exits** than any sweep predicted.

**Recommendation:** run it — a strictly-more-conservative fix to a mechanism
that was demonstrably locking losses is worth having. But add a `trail_anchor`
dimension to `simulate_symbol_day` and re-sweep before trusting any future sim
number, and watch for `TRAIL DEFERRED` lines in the first sessions.

## VPS migration — `deploy/`

Root cause of every process failure in this project: the bot only lives while a
laptop is open, and that laptop goes in a bag between class and practice during
market hours. 08-26/08-27 mid-day kills, 08-28 → 09-04 never started, and
09-08's 2h56m freeze (a 4-second retry backoff that spanned 12:01→14:57) are
one cause, not eight.

The danger is specific: bracket and native trailing stops live at Alpaca and
survive a sleeping laptop. **The EOD flatten does not** — it is a loop at
~15:50 ET. Asleep at 15:50 with a position open is the AHCO carry (−37.37,
≈3.6× intended risk).

`deploy/` holds `README-VPS.md`, `vps-setup.sh` (one-shot root installer),
`start.sh`, `notify.sh`, `healthcheck.sh` and five systemd units. Host timezone
**must** be `America/New_York`. `healthcheck.sh` at 09:45 covers the gap
`invariants.py` is structurally blind to — a day that never started.

**Arming policy inverted:** the bot now trades every market weekday unless
`state/DISARMED` exists. Opt-in arming is what cost six sessions. **Revisit
before funding real money.**

**KNOWN GAP, still open:** `run.py` catches KeyboardInterrupt, logs, and exits
**without closing positions**. `systemctl stop` mid-session leaves the exact
08-26/08-27 mess. The unit sends SIGINT so the kill is at least logged. Real
fix: a signal handler calling `_flatten_all_verified` before exit.
